# Electrical contacts

Electro Spirit (Voltorb) and base Electro Dragon (Zapdos) deliver each chained hit on arrival, including damage, stun, visual contact and audio. A launched chain retains its source identity after the source disappears. Pause freezes the queue; snapshots retain its target IDs, visited targets, last point and next movement time. Each arriving link has a distinct attack ID.

The base-card projectile speeds remain 480 and 960 world units/s, respectively, with 9 and 3 total targets, 72 and 96 world-unit chain radii, and 500 ms stun. These are converted from the pinned current raw rows (`qa/reference/native-160635007-20261103.json`, Git blob `6dd5bd2272b948fd04759007fc2ba2750a58ad39`), not from evolved cards. Target eligibility, nearest-target ordering, damage, air/ground support and ranges remain unchanged.

The timing adaptation uses a 150 ms wait between links followed by projectile travel in 50 ms reference steps. For stationary targets, the interval is `150ms + ceil(distance / (speed ×50ms)) ×50ms`. The authoritative 30 Hz simulation delivers a due step at its next simulation tick, so observed timestamps may differ by less than one simulation tick. The primary Dragon projectile leaves at the existing release contact and starts moving at the next reference step; the Spirit's first hit follows its existing physical leap.

Evidence: the [pinned RoyaleSim calibration ledger](https://github.com/RoyaleGym/RoyaleSim/blob/a849c29ae632e12f81442c45b0d9b2d2f2f99f15/data/calibration.json), `combat.CHAIN_HOP_DEAD_TARGET`, reports 12 base Spirit chains and 5/5 dead-target continuations on client 16.402, with next hits 3+ceil(distance/speed) 50 ms ticks apart. `combat.EVO_CHAIN_HOP_WAIT` explicitly identifies the base Dragon's three-tick wait; `combat.EVO_CHAIN_HOP_FIRST_STEP` separately discusses its four-tick chain trace. These base-card observations are distinct from the evolved behavior described by those entries. The local ledger's Git blob `1ba0c7366e286f1db333893bdd991b9f3d0439d1` matches the primary repository's pinned file. Exact relevant entries and hashes are retained in `ELECTRIC-TIMING-SOURCES.json`.

A dead target during an already chained hop consumes a slot and the chain continues from its last point. This is measured for Spirit on 16.402; sharing this behavior with base Dragon follows the reference engine and is an inference, not a direct Dragon measurement. A dead primary target cancels its initial shot. A living target that becomes hidden or otherwise ineligible is not hit. The raw 16.635 rows supply the current numbers; the timing traces are from older client 16.402 and are not represented as direct measurements of 16.635.

Lightning remains three independent sky strikes 500 ms apart after its existing 500 ms cast preparation. Its 1500 ms life, 500 ms interval and 500 ms offset do not add a second cast delay. Zap is an instant area hit; Electro Wizard's two hands remain simultaneous. No global electrical delay or cosmetic-only delayed damage is used.
